The Tape

[01]

The on-chain record every NEO hook reads. An issuer's attester signs each event; anyone may post it with a bond, and anyone may challenge it during the dispute window. Finalized events become plain storage and a leaf in an append-only Merkle tree. A hook calls isHalted() or action(); nothing in the swap path waits on an oracle.

CorporateActionHook

[02]

A pool bound to a symbol refuses swaps and new liquidity while the stock is halted, and from a split or rename's effective time until the pool operator acknowledges it. Removing liquidity is never blocked. Uniswap v4 reads a hook's permissions from the lowest fourteen bits of its address; enter any hook address to read them.
