The Tape: the record every hook reads

Each event is posted with the issuer's attester signature and a bond, held for a dispute window anyone can challenge, then finalized into plain storage and an append-only Merkle tree. Hooks call isHalted() and action(); nothing in the swap path waits on an oracle.

CorporateActionHook: enforcement in the pool

A pool bound to a symbol refuses swaps and new liquidity while the stock is halted, and from a split or rename's effective time until the pool operator acknowledges it. Removing liquidity is never blocked. Uniswap v4 reads a hook's permissions from the lowest fourteen bits of its address; paste any hook to read them.
