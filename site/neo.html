Overview

Corporate actions, on-chain, for tokenized equity pools.

NEO is a corporate-action oracle and a set of Uniswap v4 hooks for Robinhood Chain. Tokenized equities split, pay dividends, halt and change symbols; a pool has no record of any of it. The Tape contract records each event, attested by the issuer and open to challenge, and NEO hooks enforce it in the swap path with a single storage read.

Uniswap v4 reads a hook's permissions from the lowest fourteen bits of its address, so a hook must be deployed via CREATE2 at a salt that yields exactly those bits.
