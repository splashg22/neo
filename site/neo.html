About the project

The stock's rulebook, as a hook.

NEO is a corporate-action oracle and hook foundry for Uniswap v4 on Robinhood Chain. Tokenized stocks split, pay dividends, get halted and change symbols, and no pool on the chain knows any of that. NEO writes those events on-chain as the Tape and ships the hooks that read it.

The foundry

Pick the hooks. NEO mines the address allowed to run them
